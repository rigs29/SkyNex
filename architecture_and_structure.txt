4. METHODOLOGY & PROCESS FOR IMPLEMENTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Step 1] Lot-Relative Dynamic Anomaly Detection (Module A)
  * Dynamic computation per manufacturing lot rather than static global limits.
  * Applies 3 complementary anomaly algorithms simultaneously:
    1. Z-Score (|Z| > 3.0 relative to lot mean and standard deviation)
    2. Interquartile Range (IQR outside Q1 - 1.5*IQR to Q3 + 1.5*IQR)
    3. Isolation Forest (Unsupervised tree ensemble modeling anomaly density)

[Step 2] Early Burn-In Time-Series Drift Forecasting (Module B)
  * Extracts early 0h -> 24h reading trajectories (delta_24h, pct_change_24h, slope_0_24).
  * Feeds features into pre-trained XGBoost Regressor model to predict 168h end-of-test value.
  * Evaluates predicted degradation slope against safety threshold (95th percentile).

[Step 3] Multidimensional Composite Risk Scoring
  * Risk Score = (0.45 * Anomaly Score) + (0.40 * Drift Score) + Intersection Penalty
  * Penalty (+20) applied for dual-module anomaly detection.
  * Categorizes risk into: LOW (<25), MEDIUM (25-49), HIGH (50-74), CRITICAL (75-100).

[Step 4] Explainable AI (XAI) & Action Recommendation
  * Explanation Engine auto-generates human-readable engineering root causes.
  * QA Recommendation Engine maps risk levels to actions: PASS, EXTEND_BURN_IN, RETEST, REJECT.

[Step 5] Human-in-the-Loop QA Decisioning & Audit Trail
  * QA Engineers review recommendations in Streamlit UI workspace.
  * Immutably logged in SQLite database (`qa_decisions.db`) with timestamps and notes.
